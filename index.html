Nº 03 — Technical rider

Built for the film.

Not for the crowd.

Screen
7.3 m acoustically transparent, 2.39:1 masking. Every seat sits inside the reference viewing angle.
Projection
4K RGB laser, 120 fps, HDR. A restored 35 mm carbon-arc projector for the purists.

Dolby Atmos 9.1.4 across 22 speakers. So precise you can hear a pin drop in row C.

Nº 04 — The House

Six rooms. One evening.

At this point we ask guests to let their eyes adjust. House lights down.

I

The Auditorium

A star-field ceiling of 1,400 fibre-optic points, walnut panelling, and twelve seats with a perfect sightline from every one.

Capacity 12 · Currently 0

II

The Emerald Lounge

Green marble, brass and part of our poster collection. Champagne on arrival, cocktails at the interval, and a bar that stays open as late as you like.

Our bar staff are the only people in the lounge.

III

The Projection Booth

Where laser meets carbon arc. Our projectionist has been with the Marlowe since opening night in 1926. He knows every reel by heart.

IV

The Long Corridor

Ninety feet of original velvet linking the lounge to the auditorium. The sconces are hand-blown Murano glass. Guests often say they feel transported.

Please don't run in the corridor. He doesn't like it when you run.

V

The Powder Room

Art deco tile and a gilded mirror original to the building. Recently refurbished. Mostly.

VI

The Archive

Four thousand reels, including the only surviving print of Giggles (1926). Costumes from the original production are kept here. All of them.

Staff only. ████ ███ ████ ██ █████.
