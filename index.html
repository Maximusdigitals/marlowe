Tour the Marlowe

Six rooms.
One evening.

I

The Auditorium

A star-field ceiling of 1,400 fibre-optic points, walnut panelling, and twelve seats with perfect sightlines from every one.

Capacity 12 · Currently 0

II

The Emerald Lounge

Green marble, brass, and a vintage poster collection. Champagne on arrival, cocktails at the interval, and a bar that stays open as late as you like.

III

The Projection Booth

Where laser meets carbon arc. Our projectionist has been with the Marlowe since opening night in 1926. He knows every reel by heart.

IV

The Long Corridor

Ninety feet of original velvet linking the lounge to the auditorium. The sconces are hand-blown glass from Murano. Guests often say they feel transported.

Please don't run in the corridor. He doesn't like it when you run.

V

The Powder Room

Art deco tile and a gilded mirror original to the building. Recently refurbished. Mostly.

VI

The Archive

Four thousand reels, including the only surviving print of Giggles (1926). Costumes from the original production are stored here. All of them.
